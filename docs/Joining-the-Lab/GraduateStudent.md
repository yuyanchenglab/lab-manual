# {{page.title}}

**Owner:** Marlene

The specific process for rotating or joining a lab as a thesis student may depend on specific graduate program policies. Please check in with your program coordinator or chair for specific guidance. 

**General**
* Enrolled in a Penn (or affiliated) PhD program
* Onboarding is mainly a matter of requesting access — see [Lab Onboarding](../Onboarding/LabOnboarding) for the account-request email

**Rotation Students**
* Discuss the duration and dates of rotation with Dr. Cheng
* Confirm rotation with graduate program per program policy

**Thesis Students**
* If you are joining the Cheng Lab as a thesis student, inform your graduate program coordinator and chair
* Complete any paperwork required by graduate program given to you by your coordinator. For BGS students this includes the mentor compact which needs to be signed by Dr. Cheng.

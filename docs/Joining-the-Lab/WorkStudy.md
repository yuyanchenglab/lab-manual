**Owner:** Rushil

* As an undergraduate student, you are only allowed to bill 20 hours a week across ALL your jobs at Penn while school is in session. This does not breaks (unless you are taking classes in the summer)

**Work study**
* Onboarding is mainly a matter of requesting access — see [Lab Onboarding](../Onboarding/LabOnboarding) for the account-request email
* If Penn Financial Services has *allotted* work study funding to you as part of your aid, that is an easy way for the lab to pay you without spending their own money. You can check if you have work study funding by going to Path@Penn > My Financial Aid.
* If you do have funding, let Dr. Cheng know so she can contact HR to set you up with a work-study position in the lab.
* For fall and spring semesters, you are automatically allotted work study funding. Usually, Penn reaches out in the spring semester with a form to apply for summer work study funding. That is a simple process, and we recommend doing it if you plan to continue research in the summer!
* You can always appeal to get work study funding (or to get your allotment increased). The Penn Financial Services office is the Franklin Building on 3451 Walnut Street. I (Rushil) have had better experiences in person as opposed to via email or phone.

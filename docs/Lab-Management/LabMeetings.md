# {{page.title}}

The lab runs meetings at three levels:

1. **Weekly group meeting** — the whole lab, on the standing schedule kept in Drive: [Cheng Lab Meeting Schedule](https://drive.google.com/drive/folders/14vz73YlkqrPddrJ4QjaVDg2nH25jYi0t).
2. **Project-specific meetings** — smaller, project-team meetings separate from the general lab meeting. Cadence and format vary by project — discuss with Dr. Cheng.
3. **1:1 meetings** — individual meetings with Dr. Cheng. Discuss scheduling directly with her; see [Lab Onboarding](../Onboarding/LabOnboarding) for how to set up your first one.

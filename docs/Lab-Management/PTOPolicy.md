## Graduate Students

**Owner:** Marlene

Graduate students should communicate any time off (Sick Time, Vacation, etc.) to Yuyan as soon as possible both in-person and in writing (Slack/Email) and add it to the lab Google Calendar.*

Generally, time off for graduate students is flexible and arranged with their thesis advisor once they’ve joined the lab. Additional academic policies may vary by graduate group. For example, BGS 1st and 2nd years not covered by the CBA are still guaranteed at least 2 weeks of vacation. Graduate students covered by the [Collective Bargaining Agreement](https://getup-uaw.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/UAW-5124-CBA-2026%E2%80%932028-ocr.pdf) are guaranteed the following time off:

### Holidays (Article 13):
* Graduate Workers follow the University academic holiday schedule. If Graduate Workers are required to work on a University holiday, they should arrange to take an alternative day off approved in advance by their supervisor. Schedule adjustments can be requested to observe holidays not recognized by the University. 

### Leaves (Article 18):
* **Sick Time:** Sick time is flexible and should be communicated to the Graduate Workers advisor with as much advance notice as possible. Reasons for sick time include own illness, medical appointments, family members illness, etc. 
* **Medical Leave:** Graduate Workers are eligible for up to 6 weeks of paid medical leave subject to approval by the PhD Student Leave of Absence Policy. 
* **Bereavement Leave:** Graduate workers are entitled to bereavement leave for the loss of immediate or extended family. Please see the CBA for details. 
* Additional information on Jury Duty, Military Leave, Family and Parent Leaves, etc. please see Article 18 of the CBA. 

### Vacation (Article 31):
* Graduate workers should coordinate time off for vacation with their immediate supervisor. Additional paid vacation time beyond the days guaranteed in this article may be arranged with their supervisor. 
* Graduate workers in research positions are guaranteed a minimum of 10 paid vacation days per year. These days will not rollover or be paid out.
* Additionally Graduate workers are granted the [University Special Winter Vacation Days](https://www.hr.upenn.edu/policies-and-procedures/policy-manual/time-off/special-winter-vacation). If they are required to work during this time, they will receive equivalent time off which must be taken within 90 days. 

*Note: Some graduate programs may have processes to request certain time off in Workday but this is not processed by Yuyan.

## Postdocs

**Owner:** Gaby

UPenn Postdoc PTO Policy: 
* In any appointment year, a postdoctoral trainee may take up to 10 University business days as paid vacation days, during which the postdoctoral trainee will continue to receive their stipend or salary.
* Postdoctoral trainees are also entitled to the official holidays observed by the University and are eligible for the University’s special winter vacation, during which the University is typically closed.
* If a postdoctoral trainee works or performs research during an official University holiday or special winter vacation, the postdoctoral trainee may use the equivalent time as additional paid vacation days with mentor approval.
* Unused vacation days, holidays, or equivalent days are forfeited at the end of the appointment year and are not paid out.
* Postdoctoral trainees should discuss vacation, holidays, winter break, and any mentor-approved scheduling flexibility with their faculty mentor in advance. Departments or schools may have local procedures for recording or approving time away.

See [Vacation and Mentor-Approved Flexibility of Observed Holidays and Winter Break](https://weblogin.pennkey.upenn.edu/idp/profile/SAML2/POST/SSO?execution=e1s1) for more information

To request PTO or sick leave:
1. Login to [Penn Workday](https://weblogin.pennkey.upenn.edu/idp/profile/SAML2/POST/SSO?execution=e1s1)
2. Navigate to Personal -> Request Absence (a calendar will appear)
3. Select date/range of dates and define type of leave (PTO, Sick-self, Sick-family)
4. Submit request to Yuyan for approval*

*Requests for PTO should be made at least one month in advance and communicated to Yuyan in-person

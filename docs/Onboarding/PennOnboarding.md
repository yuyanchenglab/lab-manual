# {{page.title}}

This page covers the university-side process for permanent staff joining Penn. For lab-specific access (space, Slack, GitHub, Drive), see [Lab Onboarding](LabOnboarding). If you're temporary staff, a postdoc, or a student, see your category's requirements on [Joining the Lab](../Joining-the-Lab/) instead.

## Permanent Staff

The general process for onboarding staff at the University of Pennsylvania (UPenn) can be found here: [Onboard@Penn](https://www.onboard.upenn.edu/). This page catalogues any hitches that may not be explained through that resource.

### Virtual Tasks: Part 1

After signing your offer letter, have your reference send a recommendation letter on your behalf — this may take time depending on your reference's availability.

You'll receive an email to approve a background check. If it takes more than a week, reach out to Dr. Cheng.

You'll receive an email from `noreply@upenn.edu` to set up your PennKey. Keep note of your Penn ID number at the end of that email.

Through [Penn Workday](https://www.workday.upenn.edu/), submit your SSN and birthdate. Within the day, you should have a list of virtual paperwork to complete: Setting up your PennKey, Demographic and Contact data, Part 1 of the I-9, Disability Self-Identification, Payment Election Enrollment Event, Payroll Payment Acknowledgement, Preferred Name Changes, Personal Info Changes, Veteran Status Identification. Workday will also present other documents about employee rights that require acknowledgment.

### In-Person Task

Once the background check is complete, schedule an appointment at [Onboard@Penn](https://www.onboard.upenn.edu/) to complete part 2 of the I-9. US citizens only need a passport. Location: 2nd floor of the UPenn bookstore.

### Virtual Tasks: Part 2

You should also start your virtual Workday onboarding lessons — HIPAA and blood/sharp exposure are required for this lab (wet or dry). Each lesson can take up to 30 minutes.

Penn will assign a number of virtual onboarding lessons, including several CITI-training modules. When finished, sign up for the mandatory in-person onboarding session (~4 hours, required within your first few months).

### First Day Tasks

Get your PennCard at the card office next to Onboard@Penn. Any unfinished Workday tasks (besides lessons) will need to be resolved first.

Connect to campus wifi: [AirPennNet setup](https://www.isc.upenn.edu/how-to/connect-airpennnet-and-joinnow)

Once you have your PennCard, continue to [Lab Onboarding](LabOnboarding) for building/lab access.

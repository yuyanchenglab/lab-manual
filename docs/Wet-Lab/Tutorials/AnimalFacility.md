Our mice are primarily housed in John Morgan (JM3) – Room 363. 
Mice with planned or prior exposure to BSL2 agents are currently housed JMB 364. See BSL2 procedures below for further details on how to transfer mice to BSL2.

## 1. PPE Requirements

### JMB Room 363 (BSL1)
* Before entering Room 363: sterile gloves.
* If opening cages, also: shoe covers, isolation gown, face mask, scrub cap (optional).
* Remove all PPE before leaving the animal facility. 

### JMB Room 364 (BSL2)
**BSL2 rooms must be entered LAST during the day. If you enter a BSL2 room you CANNOT enter a BSL1 room until the next day**
* Before entering Room 264: sterile gloves, shoe covers, isolation gown.
* If opening cages, also: double gloves, face mask, scrub cap (optional).
* Remove all PPE before leaving the animal facility. 

## 2. Biosafety Cabinet Protocol

Always use the biosafety cabinet when opening cages to handle mice:
* **Before use:** turn on the light and fan.
* **During use:** the alarm rings periodically — press the alarm button to silence it.
* **After use:** wipe down the surface with disinfectant, turn off the light and fan.

## 3. Transporting Mice to the Lab

* **Survival surgery:** new/clean cages, covered with an isolation gown, water bottle flipped upside down to prevent flooding, use a cart for multiple cages.
* **Terminal surgery:** food pails, max 2–3 mice per pail.
* **General:** discard cages at the wash station when no longer needed; deactivate cage cards once a cage is no longer in use.

## 4. Breeding and Weaning Mice

***IDs:** >1000 typically denotes breeders; <1000 usually denotes pups.
***Breeder cages:** box the cage ID (e.g., C001); replace breeders every 6–8 months, sacrifice remaining pups as necessary.
***Pregnancy/weaning:** move other mice out of a pregnant female's cage, return once pups are weaned. Submit cage card requests through POLARIS at least 2 business days ahead of the weaning date. Wean at 21 days, ear-tag, update the [Cheng Lab Colony Sheet](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1nWXFYK2RldgJ07-j5yEXCYFKmiCouoic/edit?gid=868474274#gid=868474274). If you don't know which female is the mom, leave both in the cage. Check the colony regularly for new pups.
***Post-weaning:** label cage cards with breeder cage ID, mouse IDs, gender. Separate pups by gender. Max 5 mice/cage. Different litters of the same genotype can share a cage. Keep the lab at ~20 cages or fewer.

## 5. Experimental Use of Mice

Mice are used for experiments at 6–8 weeks. Use the colony sheet to select mice of the right age/genotype, mark them under "Notes" with the experiment name, keep the colony sheet current, and record mouse ID + cage card ID in the Mice Log Sheet once taken.

## 6. Animal Transfer to BSL2
**Animals must be transferred to a BSL2 housing room BEFORE exposure to any BSL2 agents (eg. Lentivirus)**
To transfer animals a BSL2 housing room (JM3 364):
* Ensure the lab has completed a [hazard briefing](https://ular.upenn.edu/form-page/annual-animal-hazard-briefing-form/) with ULAR staff within the last year
* Fill out the [rodent transfer request form] (https://ular.upenn.edu/form-page/rodent-relocation-only/) 
* Once the request has been approved, move the indicated cages to JM3 364
* After exposure to BSL2 agents, add an orange biohazard card to the cage (ask JM3 office staff for extra cards)
